About

Welcome to the NTN project—a cutting-edge solution leveraging advanced reinforcement learning and simulation to optimize urban traffic flow, reduce congestion, and enhance mobility.

Our Mission

We're committed to developing intelligent traffic systems that empower cities with data-driven decisions. By optimizing signal phases, we aim to provide scalable solutions to modern urban challenges.

How It Works

Data Collection

Gathering data from real-world inspired sources and simulated environments.

Simulation (SUMO)

Modeling realistic traffic scenarios based on the given data to replicate urban conditions.

AI Optimization

RL model analyzes patterns and optimizes signal phase changes to cut congestion.

Visualization

Display results and insights via an intuitive web interface.

Key Features

High-Fidelity Simulations
